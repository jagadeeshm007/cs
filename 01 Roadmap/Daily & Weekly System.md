# ⏱️ Daily & Weekly System (~40–45 hrs/week)

## Weekday template (Mon–Fri, ~7.5 h)
| Time | Block | What |
|---|---|---|
| 07:00–09:00 | 🧩 **DSA (Java)** | 3–4 problems in the week's pattern. 25 min timebox per medium before looking at hints. Log each one with the *DSA Problem* template |
| 09:00–09:30 | 🔁 Review | Anki + yesterday's notes (spaced repetition) |
| 10:00–12:30 | 📖 **Core study + ⚙️ internals** | Book chapter / docs for the week's topic + one internals section with its "🔬 Prove it" experiment. **Notes in your own words** in `Notes/` |
| 14:00–16:30 | 🛠️ **Build** | The week's 🎯 assignment (Orbit) or 🔬 lab. Start every experiment with a written **prediction** ([[Cognitive Drills]]) |
| 16:30–17:00 | ✍️ Journal | Daily note: what I learned, what confused me, one question to research |

## Weekend
- **Saturday (~5 h):** 1 system design case study (45-min timed, then compare with a reference) · 🧩 **pattern kata** (90 min, [[Design Patterns Roadmap]]) · 🧠 blank-page design + constraint flip · LeetCode biweekly contest when there is one
- **Sunday (~3 h):** LeetCode weekly contest · self-grade the assignment ([[Grading Rubric]]) · Weekly Review (template) · 7-day self-review of last week's code/ADR · plan next week · publish 1 short post

## Rules that compound
1. **Build the same day you learn.** Theory that isn't applied within 24h fades.
2. **Explain it in writing.** If you can't write a 5-line explanation in `Notes/`, you don't know it yet (Feynman).
3. **Timebox and move on.** Stuck > 45 min? Write down exactly what's blocking you, then look it up.
4. **Revisit with spaced repetition.** Tag hard problems/concepts `#revise`; redo them on day 3, 7, and 21.
5. **Measure, don't guess.** Track problems solved, contest rating, and hours in the weekly review.
6. **No tutorial hell.** Max 30% watching, 70% doing.
7. **Rest.** Take one half-day completely off. Burnout costs more than a skipped block.
